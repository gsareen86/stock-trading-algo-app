# Idea Discovery Workflow

Follow this disciplined top-down research funnel for Indian listed equities:

1. **Macro & Sector Rotation First (`sector_rotation`):**
   Inspect the NSE sector RRG quadrant map (`Leading`, `Improving`, `Weakening`, `Lagging`).
   Prioritise sectors rotating from `Lagging -> Improving` or holding `Improving -> Leading` with rising `RS-Momentum`.

2. **Catalyst & Theme Corroboration (`news_research`, `web_search`, `filings_scan`):**
   Look for multi-company policy tailwinds, order-book surges, import-substitution / PLI triggers, or capacity expansion cycles across RSS feeds, exchange filings, and web search.
   Expand upstream/downstream supply chains via `theme_chain`, `tier_decompose`, and `theme_participants` — keeping `established`, `claimed`, and `unestablished` exposure distinct.

3. **Technical Base & Quality Cross-Check (`setup_screener`, `company_quality`, `portfolio_and_watchlist`):**
   Surface Nifty Total Market 750 stocks with deterministic strategy Trade Plans (`Entry`, `Stop`, `Target`, `R:R`) inside those sectors. Check individual quality facets (`ROE`, `Operating Margin`, `Sales/EPS YoY`, `Debt/Equity`, marking financial leverage `not_applicable` on banks/NBFCs).
   Highlight alignment with open positions and watchlist items without inventing any blended score or recommendation.
