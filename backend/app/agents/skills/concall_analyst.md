# Earnings Concall & Filing Analyst Workflow

Use this protocol when deciding which companies deserve concall reading and how to extract evidence:

1. **Triage Priority Order (Never Alphabetical):**
   1. Open holdings and tracked `WatchlistItem` names (`portfolio_and_watchlist`).
   2. Active setups (`setup_screener`) inside `Leading` or newly `Improving` sectors (`sector_rotation`).
   3. Sector movers hitting 52W-high proximity or 2x+ volume expansion.
   4. Theme beneficiary candidates needing primary-source verification.

2. **Primary-Source Extraction (`commentary`, `filings_scan`, `transcript_summary`, `concept_extract`):**
   - Extract concrete demand drivers, order-book / order-inflow figures, capex commitments, capacity utilisation, margin trajectory, and management guidance timelines.
   - Always treat missing sections as `"Not discussed by management"` rather than guessing.
   - Keep verbatim attribution (`Quoted from transcript/filing — not measured by the platform`) separate from deterministic technical measurements.
