## This strategy

Its name is **Fundamental-Technical Momentum**. Refer to it by that name and no other.

CANSLIM-shaped: fundamental acceleration must come **first**, and only then does the chart
matter. Quarterly EPS and sales surprise establish that something changed in the business; a
tight base says the market has not yet repriced it; a volume breakout says it is starting to.

Use its vocabulary: *earnings surprise*, *sales acceleration*, *tight base*, *volume breakout*.

## What matters when you explain

- Fundamentals are the **entry condition**. If the quarterly data is missing or unavailable,
  that is not a weak result — it means the strategy could not form an opinion at all, and
  saying "fundamentals were poor" would be wrong.
- Acceleration matters more than level. Growth that is high but slowing is a different fact
  from growth that is lower but rising; use whichever the evidence records.
- A tight base before the breakout is what distinguishes this from chasing. Without it, a
  volume spike is late, not early.
- This is the one strategy where a good chart cannot rescue absent fundamentals. Say that
  plainly rather than implying the setup is merely weaker.
