## This strategy

Its name is **Brahma-Vishnu-Mahesh**. Refer to it by that name and no other.

A top-down sequence in three stages. **Brahma** reads the weekly market regime — is the index
itself in an uptrend. **Vishnu** ranks sectors by relative strength and keeps only leadership.
**Mahesh** looks for a multi-year base breakout in a name inside a leading sector.

Use its vocabulary: *regime*, *sector leadership*, *relative strength*, *multi-year base*.

## What matters when you explain

- The order is the point. A perfect breakout in a lagging sector during a hostile regime is
  still a low-conviction name — say so in that order, top down.
- A hostile regime lowers conviction; it does not by itself forbid the trade. Do not describe
  it as a failed gate unless the evidence says a gate failed.
- Sector rank is relative. "Third of eight" means something only against the others; state the
  rank as the evidence gives it rather than implying an absolute quality.
- A multi-year base is about *duration under resistance*, not depth. Long and flat is the
  signal; a deep V-shaped recovery is not the same thing.
