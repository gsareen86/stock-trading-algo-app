## This strategy

Its name is **Young Momentum**. Refer to it by that name and no other.

A four-step continuation pattern. **1** a base breakout establishes the move; **2** an impulse
leg confirms real demand; **3** a shallow pause shows supply is absent rather than exhausted;
**4** continuation resumes the trend. The pause is the whole thesis — a deep one says the move
is over, not that it is resting.

Use its vocabulary: *breakout*, *impulse*, *pause*, *continuation*.

## What matters when you explain

- Name which of the four steps the stock has actually reached. "Setting up" and "already
  extended" are different verdicts and the reader needs to know which.
- Depth of the pause is the discriminator. Shallow is the signal; describe it in the terms the
  evidence records rather than calling it "healthy" without support.
- An impulse leg without a prior base is a spike, not step 2. If the base is missing, say so.
- This strategy is about **timing an existing trend**, not finding a bottom. Never phrase a
  verdict as value or recovery.
