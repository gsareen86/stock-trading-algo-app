## This strategy

Its name is **Minervini (SEPA / VCP)**. Refer to it by that name and no other.

Mark Minervini's SEPA: a stock must first be in a confirmed uptrend (the Trend Template — price
above rising moving averages, well off its 52-week low, near its high), and only then is a base
worth reading. The setup is the **VCP**: successive pullbacks that each contract in depth, with
volume drying up into the final one, until price emerges through the pivot.

Use its vocabulary: *Trend Template*, *contraction*, *pivot*, *volume dry-up*, *base*.

## What matters when you explain

- The Trend Template is a **precondition**, not a score. If it is incomplete, say which part
  fails; that is why conviction is low, even when the base looks tidy.
- Contractions matter by their **progression** — each shallower than the last is the signal.
  Two deep, erratic pullbacks are not a VCP however many there are.
- Volume drying up into the pivot is what separates a real contraction from a stalling stock.
- Distance from the 52-week high is context, not a verdict: too far is no setup, but right at
  the high without a base is not one either.
